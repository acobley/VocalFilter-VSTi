Installed

Start your DAW and rescan its plug-ins if it does not do that by itself. VocalFilter appears as an effect by AE Cobley. Put it after something with harmonics — a pad, a saw, a drum loop, a voice. It has nothing to work on in front of a sine.

Two deliberate behaviours, so neither reads as a fault:

  • While a vowel button is lit, the nine formant sliders do nothing. Vowel is a mode, not a recall: the preset’s own values are what you hear. Touch any slider and it takes the preset’s values over and switches back to Manual, so nothing jumps at the handover.
  • A Glide of zero still takes 20 ms. An instant jump of F2 from 870 Hz to 2290 Hz is an audible click, so the shortest glide is floored just above it.
